Meet Abhinandhan

Yoga Acharya • District Yoga Competition Champion • Naturalist

Abhinandhan was born and raised in the tranquil heart of Malnad in Thirthahalli. From early childhood, surrounded by the rainforests and sacred rivers of the Western Ghats, he embraced yoga not merely as physical postures, but as a discipline of life, mind control, and self-realization.

Through years of rigorous sadhana under esteemed traditional gurus, Abhinandhan earned official certifications and proved his mastery on competitive stages by winning gold and champion titles at district-level yoga championships.

His approach blends classical Patanjali Ashtanga principles, precision Hatha alignment, therapeutic Pranayama, and lifestyle harmony. He teaches practitioners of all ages — from young students striving for competitive excellence to working professionals and seniors seeking flexibility, peace, and pain relief.

"Yoga unifies the inner self with the breath. Pure honey fuels the temple of the body with nature's pure solar energy. Together, they create Yujamrutha."

— Abhinandhan

The Magic of Kotikoppa & Araga

Thirthahalli is blessed with mist-covered valleys, mineral-rich soil, and an untouched canopy of tropical Western Ghats forests. In Kotikoppa and Araga, our apiaries are kept amidst chemical-free farm groves and wild forest borders.

Here, bees are never fed artificial sugar water or syrups. Instead, they feast organically on:

  • Wild Forest Berries & Bloom
  • Coffee & Cardamom Blossoms
  • Medicinal Malnad Shrubs
  • Teak & Wild Jasmine Flowers
