UNCOMPROMISING PURITY

The 4 Pillars of Yujamrutha Honey Purity

Pure raw honey is a living medicine. We honor the bees and protect the delicate medicinal bio-compounds in every single jar.

1

100% Raw & Cold-Extracted

Honey is extracted manually using centrifugal spinners without boiling or heating. All living enzymes (invertase, diastase) remain intact.

2

Zero Sugar / Invert Syrups

We never feed our bees artificial corn syrup or sugar water. The bees forage purely on pristine Western Ghats blossoms across Kotikoppa, Araga.

3

Sterilized Glass Packaging

Raw honey is mildly acidic and reacts with cheap plastics. We bottle only in sterilized food-grade glass jars to preserve the delicate aroma and medicinal potency.

4

Ethical Bee Conservation

We only harvest surplus honey after ensuring our bee colonies have ample honey for their sustenance and seasonal brood nourishment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Delivery & Raw Honey FAQs

Common questions about ordering, courier transit, storage, and natural crystallization.

How is the honey delivered and how safe is the glass jar?

We ship nationwide using trusted courier partners (India Post Speed Post, DTDC, Professional Couriers). Every glass jar is wrapped in multi-layer shockproof thermocol bubble-cushioning and sealed securely in corrugated outer boxes.

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed via WhatsApp are dispatched within 24 hours from Thirthahalli. Delivery within Karnataka takes 2 to 3 business days; other Indian states take 4 to 6 business days.

Why does raw honey sometimes crystallize or become thick?

Crystallization is the hallmark of 100% genuine, unheated raw honey! Commercial honey doesn't crystallize because it has been ultra-filtered to strip pollen and heated to extreme heat. Simply place your glass jar in warm water (under 40°C) for 10 minutes to return it to liquid state.

Can I pick up honey directly from the farm in Kotikoppa?

Yes! Visitors are warmly welcome at our Kotikoppa apiary in Araga, Thirthahalli. You can witness our bee boxes and collect freshly bottled honey in person. Please message us on WhatsApp (9880781965) beforehand.
