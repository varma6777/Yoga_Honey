Our Story & Vision

The Sacred Union of 'Yuj' and 'Amrutha'

Abhinandhan • Certified Yoga Instructor • District Champion

Founded by Abhinandhan, a passionate and certified yoga instructor who has won district-wide competitions, Yujamrutha represents two pillars of health: traditional yoga practice ('Yuj') and divine natural nourishment through raw farm honey ('Amrutha').

Based in the lush biodiversity of Kotikoppa, Araga in Thirthahalli, our farm honey is ethically harvested from honeybees pollinating an array of wildflowers, coffee blossoms, and medicinal herbs native to the Western Ghats.

Why Multifloral Farm Honey?

Unlike mono-floral or commercial ultra-filtered honey, our bees forage freely on diverse wild plants across Araga and Thirthahalli. This natural diversity yields a rich amber color, complex floral aroma, and superior concentration of pollen, enzymes, and antioxidants.

Our Environmental Impact

Support Sustainable Beekeeping

Honeybees are the unsung guardians of the Western Ghats ecosystem. By foraging across coffee plantations, wild teak forests, and herbal orchards in Kotikoppa and Araga, bees pollinate over 75% of local flowering flora.

When you purchase Yujamrutha honey, you directly support ethical beekeeping that protects bee colonies, preserves native biodiversity in Thirthahalli, and empowers local agricultural livelihoods.
