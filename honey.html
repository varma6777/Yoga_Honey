Consumer Guide

How to Identify 100% Genuine Honey

Simple, honest facts you can test in your own kitchen.

1. The Water Dispersion Test

Drop a spoonful of Yujamrutha honey into a transparent glass of water. Pure, dense raw honey will sink directly to the bottom in a golden swirl without instantly dissolving. Adulterated sugar syrup dissolves quickly into the water.

2. Does Pure Honey Crystallize? (Myth Buster!)

YES! Crystallization is proof of genuine, raw, unheated honey. Because pure honey contains natural pollen particles and natural glucose, it will crystallize over time in cool weather. Simply place the glass jar in a bowl of warm water (below 40°C) to return it to liquid gold!

3. Delivery & Courier Availability

We safely pack our glass jars in shock-absorbent eco-friendly packaging. We offer direct pickup at our apiary in Kotikoppa, Araga, Thirthahalli, as well as fast courier shipping across Karnataka (Bengaluru, Shivamogga, Mangaluru, Mysuru, Udupi) and all over India.
